# Executive Decision Memo

**Classification: Portfolio scenario — illustrative, not an actual employer decision.**

## Decision

**Select Tool B as the enterprise AI coding assistant**, subject to a governance remediation plan prior to full-scale rollout.

## Why

Tool B scores highest overall (4.45 vs. 4.35 for Tool A, 3.85 for Tool C) on a weighted evaluation covering developer experience, security, enterprise integration, governance, and cost. It provides the strongest combination of developer experience and enterprise integration — the two factors most predictive of whether enterprise-wide adoption actually happens — while remaining competitive on security and cost.

## The Tradeoff We're Accepting

Tool A scored higher on Security (5 vs. 4) and Governance (5 vs. 4). We are accepting somewhat greater governance complexity in exchange for materially stronger developer experience and integration. This tradeoff is not being ignored — it is tracked as an active mitigation item in the [Risk Register](../risk-analysis/risk-register.md), with a governance remediation plan required as a gate before the enterprise rollout phase.

## What We're Asking For

- Executive approval to proceed with a pilot (see [Rollout Plan](../rollout-plan/rollout.md)).
- Security and Risk/Compliance sign-off on the governance remediation plan before controlled expansion.
- Budget authorization scoped to the pilot phase only — enterprise-scale budget is a separate decision gate after pilot results are in.

## What Would Change This Decision

- Pilot data showing developer adoption significantly below expectations.
- Security review surfacing an issue beyond what the governance remediation plan addresses.
- A material, unfavorable change in vendor pricing or contract terms during negotiation.
